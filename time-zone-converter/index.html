Our Time Zone Converter uses the browser's built-in Intl API to handle daylight saving time (DST) automatically. All conversions happen locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server.

Whether you're scheduling a meeting across continents, planning travel, or coordinating with remote teams, this tool gives you accurate time conversions for 50+ major cities worldwide. The world clock below shows the current time in key cities, updating in real-time.

Is this time zone converter free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no watermark, no limits. All processing happens in your browser.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. The converter uses the IANA time zone database via the Intl API, which automatically accounts for DST transitions in all supported regions.

How accurate are the conversions?

Conversions are based on your browser's IANA time zone database, which is kept up to date by your operating system. Results are as accurate as your system's time zone data.

Can I use this offline?

Yes. After the page loads, all conversions work offline since everything runs in your browser using the built-in Intl API.
