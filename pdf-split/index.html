What is PDF Splitting?

PDF splitting is the process of dividing a single PDF document into multiple smaller PDFs. This is useful when you need to share only certain pages, extract specific sections, or break a large document into manageable parts. Our tool performs this operation entirely in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why Use Our Free PDF Splitter?

  • Three Split Modes: Split by page ranges, extract specific pages into one file, or split every page into individual files.
  • No Watermarks: The output PDFs are clean — no branding or watermarks.
  • No Signup Required: Start splitting immediately. No email, no account.
  • Works Everywhere: Fully responsive — works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Common Use Cases

  • Extract specific pages: Pull out pages 3-7 from a 50-page report to share only the relevant section.
  • Split large documents: Break a 100-page manual into smaller chapters for easier handling.
  • Separate forms: Split a multi-form PDF into individual files for separate submission.
  • Extract invoices: Pull individual invoices from a combined statement PDF.
  • Share partial content: Send only the pages you need without sharing the entire document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF splitter really free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. We sustain the tool through non-intrusive advertising on the page.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All splitting happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

How do I specify page ranges?

Use the format "1-3, 5, 7-10" where hyphens indicate ranges and commas separate them. Each range becomes a separate PDF file.

What happens when I split into individual pages?

Each page of your PDF becomes a separate PDF file. Since this creates multiple files, they are packaged as a ZIP for download.

What's the maximum file size?

We recommend files up to 50MB. Since processing happens in your browser, larger files may be slower.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! Our splitter works on iOS, Android, and any modern mobile browser.

Will the quality be affected?

No. Splitting simply extracts pages — no re-encoding or compression. Your pages look exactly as they did in the original.
