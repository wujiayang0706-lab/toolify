Understanding Exchange Rates

An exchange rate is the value of one currency expressed in terms of another. For example, if the USD to EUR rate is 0.92, it means 1 US Dollar can be exchanged for 0.92 Euros. Exchange rates fluctuate constantly due to factors including interest rates, inflation, political stability, economic performance, and trade balances.
