What is Word to PDF Conversion?

Word to PDF conversion turns a Microsoft Word document (.docx) into a Portable Document Format (.pdf) file. PDF is the universal standard for sharing documents because it preserves formatting and looks identical on every device — making it ideal for resumes, contracts, reports, and forms. Our converter performs this entirely in your browser, so your documents never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Word to PDF converter really free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. We sustain the tool through non-intrusive advertising on the page.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device, making this safe for confidential documents.

Does it support the old .doc format?

This tool supports the modern .docx format (Office 2007+). The legacy .doc format uses a different binary structure and is not supported here.

Will the formatting be preserved?

Text, headings, lists, tables, and basic formatting are preserved. Complex layouts, embedded fonts, or advanced Word features may render slightly differently, since conversion happens without Microsoft Word.

What's the maximum file size?

We recommend files up to 50MB. Since conversion happens in your browser, available memory is the limiting factor on very large documents.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! Our converter works on iOS, Android, and any modern mobile browser.

Are images in the document kept?

Inline images embedded in the .docx are rendered in the PDF. Images linked from external locations are not included.
