What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information — URLs, plain text, contact details, Wi-Fi credentials, and more. When scanned with a phone camera, it instantly opens the encoded content. QR codes are widely used on menus, posters, packaging, business cards, and tickets. Our generator creates them entirely in your browser, so nothing you encode is ever sent to a server.

Understanding Error Correction

QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction, which means a damaged or partially obscured code can still scan. Higher levels add redundant data so the code tolerates more damage — but make it denser. Low (7%) is fine for clean digital display; High (30%) is best for printed codes that may get scratched or dirty, or when you want to overlay a small logo in the center.
