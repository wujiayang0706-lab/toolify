    What is HEIC and Why Convert to JPG?

    HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format used by iPhone since iOS 11. It compresses photos to about half the size of JPEG at the same quality — great for storage, but a pain for compatibility. Many apps, websites, and Windows PCs can't open HEIC files natively. JPG is the universal image format that opens everywhere. Converting HEIC to JPG makes your iPhone photos usable on any device, in any app.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this HEIC to JPG converter really free?

    Yes, completely free with no limits. We sustain the tool through non-intrusive advertising on the page.

    Are my photos uploaded to a server?

    No. All conversion happens locally in your browser using the heic2any library (compiled from libheif to WebAssembly). Your photos never leave your device.

    Does it support HEIF as well as HEIC?

    Yes. HEIC is a variant of HEIF, and our converter handles both .heic and .heif files.

    Is there a quality loss?

    Conversion decodes the HEIC and re-encodes to JPEG at high quality. There is minor re-compression, but it's visually lossless for typical photos. The resolution is preserved.

    Why is conversion slower than other tools?

    Because conversion runs entirely in your browser (not a server), speed depends on your device. The first conversion may also take a moment while the library initializes. This trade-off keeps your photos private.

    What's the maximum file size?

    We recommend files up to 50MB each. Since conversion happens in your browser, available memory is the limiting factor.

    Does it work on mobile?

    Yes! Our converter works on iOS, Android, and any modern mobile browser.
